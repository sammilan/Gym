# Workouts — 5-Day Split, Monday (Day 1) to Friday (Day 5)

> Rebuilt from SKILL.md (benchmarks, knee and shoulder rules) because the original
> `workouts.md` was missing. Swap in anything that differs from what you actually do.

Every session: Warm-up → Shoulder Prehab Block → Knee Block → Main workout → Kettlebell Finisher → Cooldown Stretch.

## Blocks used every day

**Warm-up** — Bike 5 min, resistance ≤ 5 (stop if the knee complains), or incline walk 5 min.

**Shoulder Prehab Block**
- Scapular squeezes — 2×12
- Doorway chest stretch — 30 sec/side
- Band external rotation — 2×12 (light band)
- Wall slides — 2×10

**Knee Block**
- Glute bridge — 2×12
- Side-lying clamshell — 2×12/side
- Straight-leg raise — 2×10/side

**Kettlebell Finisher** — Kettlebell deadlift 2×10 + farmer carry 2×30 m. No swings or jumps.

**Cooldown Stretch** — Hamstrings, hip flexors, chest, lats: 30 sec each.

## Day 1 — Chest · Back
- Supine Press — 3×10 — 25 kg
- Lat Pulldown — 3×10 — 45 kg
- Seated Row — 3×12 — 36 kg
- Pec Fly — 2×12 — 27–32 kg
- Reverse Pec Deck — 2×15 — 23 kg

## Day 2 — Arms (Biceps · Triceps)
- Biceps Curl (machine) — 3×12 — 20 kg
- Cable Tricep Pushdown (rope) — 3×12
- DB Hammer Curl — 2×12
- Single-arm Cable Pushdown — 2×12/side
- Face Pulls — 2×15 (shoulder health)

No overhead tricep extensions or dips: both load the right shoulder in the impingement position.

## Day 3 — Cardio + Core
- Incline treadmill walk — 20 min · 3.2–3.5 mph · 4–6% incline (no running)
- Dead Bug — 3×8/side
- Bird Dog — 2×10/side
- Pallof Press — 2×10/side
- Plank — 3×30 sec

## Day 4 — Legs (knee-safe only)
- Hamstring Curl — 3×12 — 23–27 kg (priority)
- Leg Press (shallow range of motion) — 3×12
- Hip Thrust / Glute Bridge machine — 3×12
- Cable Glute Kickback — 2×12/side
- Seated Calf Raise — 2×15

NEVER leg extensions. No lunges or deep squats. Stop any movement that hurts the knee.

## Day 5 — Shoulders (scaled) + Core
- Reverse Pec Deck — 3×15 — 23 kg
- Face Pulls — 3×15
- Cable Lateral Raise (light) — 2×12
- Cable Crunch — 2×12
- Plank — 2×30 sec

Shoulder Press stays out until the prehab block is pain-free for 2 weeks in a row, then comes back at ≤ 18–19 kg. Drop any movement that pinches.
